The paper studies fixed-weight inference/training discrepancies across different
stacks and then RL behavior. Its Q/K norm–RoPE intervention retains selected
intermediates in FP32 until BF16 outputs. Figure2 also covers other regions
that this case leaves unchanged. Section6 and Table1 show that changing
precision in one region need not uniformly reduce discrepancy. Its sampled
token estimator differs from our exact full-vocabulary KL at selected
positions. Those model sizes, training outcomes and table values are not
Case021 measurements.

The official PDF is retained only in the private source-reading folder. Text,
equations and table entries were read; the web screenshot service failed, so
no claim of a successful rendered Figure2 inspection is made.

The exact Case013 files used are preserved under
[`provenance/case013_original/`](provenance/case013_original/) and indexed in
[`provenance/preservation_before.json`](provenance/preservation_before.json).
The old probe is never imported: its import-time ACTIVE_RUN lookup is replaced
with explicit CLI paths. No source solver or quantizer is executed.

Model, current artifact and runtime identities are recorded in
[`artifact_manifest.json`](artifact_manifest.json) and
[`environment.json`](environment.json). The local source bundle is identified
by [`provenance/freeze_v1.json`](provenance/freeze_v1.json), separately from the
base Git commit. Existing probe02 and probe03 used different settings and are
not treated as a controlled experiment with each other.
