Battery cycling worksheet
Purpose: Separate test conditions from acceptance decisions for rechargeable cells.
This is an invented example with no real people, facilities, or measurements. The numeric values are illustrative, not operating advice.
Operating rule: Capacity is compared at the same discharge rate and rest interval. A warm cell is allowed to cool before resistance measurements are accepted.

record_format = sectioned key-value register
review_policy = retain original observations and append corrections

observations.cutoff_voltage = 48
review_note = cell_a is inspected at its own checkpoint. Values for capacity, internal_resistance, cycle_count are recorded before the decision for this battery cycling worksheet is made.
evidence_rule = Capacity is compared at the same discharge rate and rest interval. A warm cell is allowed to cool before resistance measurements are accepted.
next_action = Compare internal_resistance with the applicable operating limit, record the result, and assign a follow-up for cell_a when evidence is incomplete.
