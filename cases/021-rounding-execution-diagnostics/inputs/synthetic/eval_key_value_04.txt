Cold chain transfer record
Purpose: Describe temperature evidence when sealed packages move between stores.
This is an invented example with no real people, facilities, or measurements. The numeric values are illustrative, not operating advice.
Operating rule: A logger gap is reviewed as missing evidence, not a compliant temperature. Receiving staff record both package condition and the time the package reached cold storage.
