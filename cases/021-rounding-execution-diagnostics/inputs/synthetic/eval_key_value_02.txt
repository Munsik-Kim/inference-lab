Equipment handover record
Purpose: Track custody and inspection for portable survey instruments.
This is an invented example with no real people, facilities, or measurements. The numeric values are illustrative, not operating advice.
Operating rule: The receiver checks the serial number and signs for physical condition separately. An intact transport box does not establish that an instrument remains calibrated.

record_format = sectioned key-value register
review_policy = retain original observations and append corrections
