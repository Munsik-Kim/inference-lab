Seed bank rotation
Purpose: Explain why seed viability and storage conditions are tracked separately.
This is an invented example with no real people, facilities, or measurements. The numeric values are illustrative, not operating advice.
Operating rule: A low germination result triggers a confirmatory assay before regeneration. Opening a vial is logged because moisture exposure changes later storage decisions.

The report separates an observation, a decision, and a follow-up. An observation records what was measured at a particular checkpoint. A decision applies the stated rule to that evidence. A follow-up identifies what must be checked next. Keeping these three fields separate makes uncertainty visible without pretending that an unobserved value was measured.
