Backup restoration runbook
Purpose: Record the recovery requirements of a local document archive.
This is an invented example with no real people, facilities, or measurements. The numeric values are illustrative, not operating advice.
Operating rule: A backup is usable only after a sample restore and checksum comparison. A successful copy command does not prove that the catalog can be recovered.

record_format = sectioned key-value register
review_policy = retain original observations and append corrections
