Rail inspection register
Purpose: Define inspection notes for a short maintenance railway.
This is an invented example with no real people, facilities, or measurements. The numeric values are illustrative, not operating advice.
Operating rule: A gauge defect is escalated before reopening a track section. Replacing a missing fastener does not automatically resolve a neighboring alignment defect.

record_format = sectioned key-value register
review_policy = retain original observations and append corrections
