# Interpretation limits

- Toy operators are designed numerical controls. They are not trained CKDA weights and do not establish long-context language-model quality.
- The learned study fixes S3, one small architecture and a bounded local training schedule. Native length extrapolation can fail before low-precision storage becomes the limiting factor.
- First-failure survival concerns every prefix. Final-quarter accuracy and recovery after an error remain separate diagnostics.
- The added four mixed-precision allocations reuse TEST under a separate post-hoc budget freeze. Joint630-family bounds account for the displayed arm/length/seed menu; they do not turn reused-TEST comparisons into independent confirmation or certify a globally optimal allocation. No residual arm extends the supported5%-risk horizon over its best feasible comparator in this tested menu; some RMST differences remain.
- The maximum observed length is2048; passing it is right-censored evidence, not infinite memory. Simultaneous confidence support can be shorter than the empirical grid estimate, especially at risk1%.
- The codec uses sequential CPU reference operations, temporary FP32 arrays and explicit packing. Serialized state reduction does not establish fused-kernel speedup or total model/allocator VRAM reduction.
- Fixed bases, configuration, scale bytes, RNG and cursor cost are charged. The task-specific exact group-ID tracker includes its table/decoder and serves a different representation class.
- DAMP and published recurrent EF are discussed from verified sources but are not claimed as exact replications. The untransported ablation is one defined update, not a substitute for all EF literature.
- No broader layer/model sweep, new language-model download, retraining per codec or TEST-dependent threshold search is part of this v1.
- This is a local review candidate. Existing DIOVA cases and unrelated private recurrent research remain separate. No remote publication or checkpoint upload is included.
